Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase a Link Reactor PRO license, we collect only the information necessary to process your order and deliver your license key:

  • Your email address — to deliver the license key
  • Payment amount and selected plan — for transaction records

All payment processing is handled securely by our payment provider. We do not store, process, or have access to your credit card details, banking information, or any sensitive financial data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used exclusively to deliver your license key after successful payment. We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional materials. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any third parties.

3. License Verification

To verify your PRO license status, the Link Reactor WordPress plugin periodically sends only your license key and website domain name to our license server. No other data from your website, its users, or its content is ever transmitted or collected.

4. Data Retention

License data is retained for the duration of your license validity plus an additional 90 days for support and verification purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time. Upon deletion, your license will be deactivated.

5. Cookies

Our website uses a single functional cookie to remember your language preference. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies of any kind.

6. Third-Party Services

We use a third-party payment processor to handle transactions securely. Please refer to their respective privacy policy for details on how they process your payment data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, please use our contact form.
